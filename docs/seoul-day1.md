## Design rules

From the script, unchanged:

- Day 1 runs from Incheon to bedtime. Every origin can arrive early by its own shortcut (always early, sets a toll) or by the language road (open to all; read the board right and you're early with no toll; miss it and one rescue decides on time or late).
- Arrival timing is early, on time or late. Each one shows the player something the other two don't.
- Origins on Day 1 change who reaches out and what's expected of you. Perks and weekly loops start on Day 2.
- The Hangul track teaches a few letters at a time, always reusing letters already met. A tracker (한글 X/24) shows progress. Eval 1 doesn't test Hangul reading; it is taught through March.
- Tong teaches in exercises and in opt-in Tong moments (the ride in, bedtime). Tong never comments on character scenes.
- Words in scenes are tap-to-gloss subtitles. Characters don't explain themselves.

## Scenes

### 1. Incheon arrivals (1:05pm)

As the script: Tong appears, the KakaoTalk from Manager Choi ("오늘 오후 3시까지 회사로 오세요."), the origin's second message.

**Exit sign (block crush).** 입구 and 출구 side by side. Rebuild 출구 to find the way out. New: ㅊ ㅜ ㄹ ㄱ (4/24).

### 2. The station (1:25pm)

> **Tong:** The agency's in Hongdae. We need the train that stops at 홍대입구.

| Road | Who | What happens | Arrival | Toll |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Read the board | Everyone | Board (below) | Early if right | None |
| Ask a stranger | Picked the express | Rescue at Seoul Station | On time or late | None |
| Taxi on the family card | International-School Kid | Driver small talk (as the script) | Early | Debt +1, about ₩70,000 |
| Run for the all-stop | Athlete | Sprint with luggage as the doors close | Early | Strain +1 (tight calf) |
| Let the fan help | Creator | A fan walks you to the right train (as the script) | Early | Exposure +1 or +2 |

**The board (block crush).** Two rows: 직통 · 서울역 · 13:30 and 일반 · 서울역 · 13:32. Rebuild the row you want.

> **Tong:** 일 is ㅇ, silent at the start, then ㅣ, an "ee", with your ㄹ underneath. 일반 stops everywhere, including Hongdae. 직 starts with ㅈ, a "j". 직통 goes straight to Seoul Station and skips us.

New: ㅇ ㅣ ㅈ (7/24). The trap: both rows share ㅣ, and the express leaves first.

**The stop (block crush).** On the train, the line map. Rebuild 홍대입구 to know where to get off. The last two blocks are the 입구 from the airport sign. New: ㅎ ㅗ ㄷ ㅂ (11/24). ㅐ in 대 is glossed but not counted.

**Rescue: ask a stranger (`sentence_builder`).** Only if you took the express. At Seoul Station, a stranger. Tiles: 실례합니다 / 홍대 / 어떻게 / 가요?

- Right: "실례합니다. 홍대 어떻게 가요?" She points you back to the all-stop, two stops. **On time.**
- Wrong, or English: sent to the taxi rank and buses. **Late.**

**Tong moment: the ride in.** As the script (formal endings, 언니 and 씨, 잘 부탁드립니다), each one marking a tile in the self-introduction.

### 3. Arrival

**Early (2:25): Haeun alone.** As the script, shorter, and she stays behind the wall: she stops the music, "...누구세요?", then "실장님 3시에 오세요." and starts the music again. Every greeting gets the same polite 네. The choice that counts is what you do next: step out (trust +1) or stay and watch. The Creator's "raise the phone" choice (trust −2) stays. Her real introduction is still H1 on day 2.

**On time (2:58): the cohort.** As the script. Do-hee's line to Yun-seo, the airport-photo line if Exposure is set, Yun-seo's 고생 많으셨죠, Da-on's wave, Haeun's glance.

**Late (3:20): Bo-ram covers.** As the script, with Manager Choi. She becomes your roommate tonight, which makes the night quieter and kinder.

**Ambient: Sua left.** The agency is still talking about it. Overheard in corridors and the studio on Day 1 and the days after. Proposed lines (placeholders until native review, #395):

- 민지: 수아 언니 진짜 나갔대. 짐도 다 뺐대. *(Su-a really left. She cleared out all her stuff.)*
- 예린: 그치? 평가 전에… *(Right? Before the eval...)*
- A trainee from another unit: 그 자리 새로 온 애가 들어간대. *(I hear the new one's taking that spot.)*
- 보람 (when someone brings it up): 됐고. 연습해. *(Enough. Practise.)*

They say that she left and when. They never say why, where she went, or anything about Haeun (T10).

### 4. Orientation (3:00pm)

1. **The rules.** Manager Choi: phones after 9pm, no social media, no dating, curfew at 11. "첫 월말평가는 이번 달 말이에요."
2. **Self-introduction (`sentence_builder`).** As the script, with formal blocks and casual decoys. The room reacts; nobody fails. Tong points at 입니다: 니 = ㄴ + ㅣ, 다 = ㄷ + ㅏ. New: ㄴ ㅏ (13/24).
3. **The origin beat.** As the script: the parents' call (International-School Kid), the airport photo or "여기선 0명이에요" (Creator), Coach Park: "운동했어요? 앞줄." (Athlete, paying off in the day 2 drill).
4. **Lockers (built H0).** Manager Choi takes you to your locker: Sua's scraped label (block crush 수아), the notebook, Haeun's glimpse. New: ㅅ (14/24). Coach Park: tomorrow, 10am, the studio.
5. **The registration card (built).**

### 5. Dorm and first night

1. Manager Choi gives you the address. The map takes you to Yeonnam Apartments.
2. **Door plates (block crush).** Your floor and room number; 호 and 층 reuse letters you know. Review only.
3. **The dorm (built `haeun.h0.dorm`).** Choi offers Sua's bed, Haeun sends you to Da-on's room, and Da-on and Bo-ram meet you.
4. **The evening.** The 기력 bar appears. Unpack and sleep early (start day 2 full), or stay up with your roommates (costs 기력; Da-on's questions, Bo-ram sending her to bed).
5. **Tong moment: bedtime.** As the script, only about things you saw: Bo-ram's 반말 (late arrival), Yun-seo's 고생 많으셨죠 (on time), 언니. Then the recap cards for the day's 14 letters, each tied to where you saw it.
