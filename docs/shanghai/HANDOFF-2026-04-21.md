## 30-second context

Shanghai onboarding = first-time hangout in the 小笼包 shop. Player walks in, is seated facing 丁漫, overhears 守成/丁漫 negotiating a show deal, 守成 takes a personal phone call and leaves after a QR-code payment; 方阿姨 closes with `瞿家的小儿子……` — the cliffhanger reveal.

**The whole scene IS the webtoon.** Not "live scene + cliffhanger panel at the end" — one continuous vertical-scroll webtoon from establishing shot through 方阿姨's reveal. Tong whispers, exercises and credit gates overlay as the player scrolls.

Monetization hooks baked into the V1 demo:

- **Per-bubble translation gate (SA paywall):** EN translation is hidden under each bubble by default; 1 SP to reveal. Pinyin ruby annotations are ALWAYS free.
- **Premium scene-unlock (Game Pass):** the 方阿姨 cliffhanger bubble (`瞿家的小儿子……`) is gated behind Game Pass — you can't even open its translation help without the entitlement.

## Goals (original intent)

1. Shanghai onboarding hangout reachable (a) directly via a playtest link, (b) as the default first hangout when a player picks Chinese as their priority language.
2. Webtoon-driven presentation — the fixture supplies panels + bubbles + layout metadata; the renderer is metadata-driven so no panel-specific CSS branches.
3. Dynamic AI in prod + fixture-verbatim in dev/QA — same fixture scaffolds both. V1 ships fixture-verbatim; dynamic follow-on is Issue #196.
4. Demo-ready monetization: per-bubble translation paywall + premium scene unlock on the reveal.
5. Voice-rule validator prevents AI cringe on NPC dialogue (`不一样的` meta-tag, invented backstory, wrong register on 方阿姨's diminutives, etc.).
